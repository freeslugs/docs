## Traditional Banking

**Connection Method**: Plaid API for secure bank-grade access to 10,000+ FDIC-insured institutions

**Major Banks**
- Chase, Wells Fargo, Bank of America, Citibank
- US Bank, PNC Bank, TD Bank, Capital One
- Plus thousands of regional and community banks

**Online Banks & Credit Unions**
- Ally Bank, Marcus (Goldman Sachs), Discover Bank, SoFi
- Chime, Varo, Current, Aspiration
- Navy Federal, Pentagon Federal, and thousands of credit unions

## Cryptocurrency Support

**Connection Method**: Public wallet addresses for receiving funds. Phantom, MetaMask, and other wallet integrations for sending transactions.

**Supported Networks**
- Bitcoin, Lightning Network
- EVM Chains: Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base
- Solana
- Other Networks: Cardano, Polkadot, Cosmos
- Stablecoins: Starting with USDC, expanding to USDT, DAI

**Centralized Exchanges**
- Coinbase (200+ cryptocurrencies), Binance.US (100+ cryptocurrencies), Kraken (90+ cryptocurrencies)
- Gemini (70+ cryptocurrencies), Crypto.com (250+ cryptocurrencies)
