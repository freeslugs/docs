## Instant Transfer

Fellow's core innovation transforms every transfer request into a short-term credit instrument, providing immediate access to your money while traditional settlement happens in the background.

**Supported Transfer Routes**

- **Bank ↔ Bank**: Instant transfers between any traditional financial institutions
- **Bank ↔ Crypto**: Move fiat to Fellow's crypto wallet or external exchanges instantly
- **Crypto ↔ Crypto**: Cross-chain transfers without manual bridging
- **Investment ↔ Any**: Instantly move funds to/from brokerage accounts
- **International**: Cross-border transfers with competitive FX rates
