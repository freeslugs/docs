## Why Banks Don't Want You to Know This

Traditional banking creates a massive profit gap that you've been locked out of:

  <Card title="How Banks Work Today" icon="building-columns">

    **Bank Lends Your Money**: Earns 20-25% on credit products
    **Bank Keeps the Difference**: 15-20% profit margin
    **You Get**: Scraps while they profit from your capital
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="How Fellow Works" icon="coins">
    **You Stake Capital**: Provide liquidity directly to users
    **Users Pay Transfer Fees**: 40-80 basis points per transaction
    **You Earn the Spread**: 30-65% APR on your staked balance
    **Fellow Takes**: Small protocol fee (10-15%)
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**The Bottom Line**: Fellow eliminates the banking middleman and lets you capture the returns that banks have been keeping from you.
