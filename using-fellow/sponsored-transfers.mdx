Get paid to discover new financial platforms. Fellow partners with emerging networks and protocols to offer cash incentives when you transfer funds to try their services.

<Info>
  **Guaranteed Earnings**: Unlike speculative airdrops, sponsored transfers offer upfront cash payments for completed transfers to partner platforms. User may also qualify for rewards from protocols for successful completion of incentivized actions.
</Info>

## How to Participate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse Available Campaigns">
    Check Fellow's sponsored transfer marketplace for current opportunities and requirements
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Platform">
    Select transfers based on earning potential, platform quality, and your risk tolerance
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Qualifying Transfer">
    Use Fellow to transfer the required amount to the partner platform
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet Campaign Requirements">
    Some campaigns require keeping funds for a minimum period or trying specific features
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Payment">
    Earn cash rewards directly in your Fellow account within 24-48 hours of completion
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  **Pro Tip**: You can often stack multiple campaigns by transferring to platforms that integrate with each other, maximizing your earnings per dollar moved.
</Check>
