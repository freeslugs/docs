## Partnership Quality & Safety

Fellow carefully vets all platform partnerships to protect users while maximizing earning opportunities.

**Our Partner Standards:**

- **Venture Backed**: Partner platforms have credible funding and teams
- **Security Audited**: All smart contracts undergo third-party security reviews
- **Legal Compliance**: Partners operate within regulatory frameworks
- **User Protection**: Clear terms for fund recovery and dispute resolution

**Risk Management:**

- Fellow maintains emergency recovery procedures for all partner platforms
- Campaign requirements never involve permanent lock-ups or irreversible actions
- User funds remain under their control throughout sponsored transfer programs
- 24/7 support for any issues with partner platform interactions

<Warning>
  **Important**: While Fellow vets partners carefully, you're still interacting with external platforms. Never transfer more than you can afford to lose, and always understand platform-specific risks.
</Warning>
