# Start Routing Your Money in Minutes

Fellow makes it simple to connect all your financial accounts and move money instantly between any platforms. If you have a phone number, you already have a Fellow account—just download the app to get started.

<Note>
  **Universal Access**: Your phone number is your universal financial identity with Fellow. No complex usernames, passwords, or account setup required.
</Note>

### System Requirements:
- **Stable internet connection** (Wi-Fi or cellular)
- **Phone number** for verification and transfers
- **Camera access** for document verification (optional)
- **Biometric authentication** recommended for security

## Step 2: Verify Your Phone Number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Your Number">
    **Phone Verification:**
    - Enter your mobile phone number
    - Choose your country code if international
    - This becomes your Fellow identity for sending/receiving money
    - Works with any carrier (Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, etc.)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm with SMS">
    **Verification Code:**
    - Receive 6-digit code via SMS
    - Enter code in Fellow app
    - Code expires in 10 minutes
    - Can request new code if needed
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Set Up Security">
    **Protect Your Account:**
    - Enable Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint
    - Create 6-digit backup PIN
    - Allow push notifications for transaction alerts
    - Review privacy and security settings
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Important**: Use your real phone number that you regularly access. This is how you'll send and receive money, and how people can find you on Fellow.
</Warning>

## Step 3: Connect Your Financial Accounts

Fellow works by securely connecting to your existing financial accounts. You don't need to move money—it stays where it is while Fellow gives you the power to route between platforms.

    1. Select your bank from the list

## Step 4: Make Your First Transfer

Once you've connected your accounts, try a small test transfer to see Fellow's instant routing in action.

### Sending Money to Someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Recipient">
    **Enter Phone Number:**
    - Type recipient's phone number
    - Select from contacts if permission granted
    - Recipient doesn't need Fellow app installed
    - Works with any US or international number
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select Amount & Source">
    **Pick Your Funding Source:**
    - See all connected account balances
    - Choose which account to send from
    - Enter dollar amount to send
    - Preview total cost including fees
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review & Send">
    **Confirm Details:**
    - Verify recipient number and amount
    - Review routing path and estimated time
    - Confirm with biometric or PIN
    - Track transfer status in real-time
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Moving Your Own Money

<Steps>
  <Step title="View All Accounts">
    **See Your Money:**
    - Dashboard shows all connected account balances
    - Real-time updates from all platforms
    - Total net worth calculation
    - Recent transaction history
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select Transfer Route">
    **Choose Source & Destination:**
    - Pick which account to move money from
    - Select destination account
    - Fellow shows optimal routing path
    - Estimated settlement time displayed

  <Step title="Execute Transfer">
    **Instant Movement:**
    - Confirm transfer details
