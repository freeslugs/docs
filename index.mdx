Fellow is the first crypto-compatible bank that functions as connective tissue for all of your financial accounts while enabling users to "become the bank." Fellow makes finance frictionless between your financial platforms and gives you complete control over your money.

## TL;DR

• **Move money instantly** between any financial account using just phone numbers, no compliance overhead  
• **Connect scattered platforms** - Apple Pay to Coinbase, Phantom to traditional banking - and get compensated through staking rewards  
• **Explore the ecosystem** - All major financial platforms, banks, and crypto wallets are available through Fellow's router  

## What is Fellow?

Fellow is a modern digital banking platform that consolidates and connects existing financial products together, agnostic of the rails they are built on. If you have a phone number, you have a Fellow account. 

We've built an entire financial stack that allows you to move money instantly between any platform that supports ACH or cryptocurrency, while simplifying finance in a world of scattered digital assets and platforms.

That means it's easy to take back control of your money - with instant transfers, easy payments, and best-in-class high-yield savings accounts. Fellow simultaneously allows users to "become the bank" by staking a portion of their total account balances to extend short-term credit to other users to power their transfers.

## What is the Router?

The Router is an ultra-short tenure credit attribution protocol that enables near-instant transfers for many commonly used account-to-account routes.

**For Users**  
The Router is an interface to easily move money between any financial platforms with instant settlement.

**For Liquidity Providers**  
Fellow serves as a 2-sided marketplace, matching the need for instant account liquidity with capital providers. The Router shows your staking rewards, earnings, and participation in the credit infrastructure.
