## What is Fellow?

Fellow is a modern digital banking platform that consolidates and connects existing financial products together, agnostic of the rails they are built on. If you have a phone number, you have a Fellow account.

We've built an entire financial stack that allows you to move money instantly between any platform that supports ACH or cryptocurrency, while simplifying finance in a world of scattered digital assets and platforms.

That means it's easy to take back control of your money - with instant transfers, easy payments, and best-in-class high-yield savings accounts. Fellow simultaneously allows users to "become the bank" by staking a portion of their total account balances to extend short-term credit to other users to power their transfers.

## What is the Transfer Router?

The Transfer Router is an ultra-short tenure credit attribution protocol that enables near-instant transfers for many commonly used account-to-account routes.

**For Users**\
Users get best-in-class, digitally native banking services such as P2P payments, high yield savings accounts, accesible access to DeFi, and instan routing between the platforms they already know and enjoy using. Fellow's \*\*Router \*\*enables users to easily move money between any two financial platforms with instant settlement.

**For Liquidity Providers**\
Fellow serves as a 2-sided marketplace, matching the need for instant account liquidity with capital providers. Credit Liquidity Providers (CLPs) can access Fellow via webapp or native iOS frontend and provide short-duration credit to ensure near-instant A2A settlement while earning attractive yields.
