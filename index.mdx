# Global finance is fragmented by platform.

**The Problem:** Your money gets trapped in disconnected silos. Each financial app, bank, and wallet operates independently with no way to communicate.

**The Reality:**
- Moving money from **Apple Pay to Coinbase** takes 5+ business days
- Traditional **cross-platform transfers cost $25-50** in fees
- **91% of consumers** use multiple financial apps that don't talk to each other
- Your money moves instantly everywhere **except between your own accounts**

Even "instant" payments aren't really instant—they're just IOUs while your money still takes 1-3 days to actually move.

## Fellow is the financial router that fixes this.

## The Technology Behind Fellow

### Router Architecture
Fellow operates as intelligent middleware that:
- **Identifies optimal paths** between any two financial platforms
- **Handles protocol translation** between traditional banking and crypto networks  
- **Manages liquidity pools** to ensure instant settlement capability
- **Abstracts complexity** so users see simple phone number transfers

### Established Infrastructure
<Note>
  **Regulatory Framework**: Fellow operates as an Authorized Delegate under BitGo's Principal License Holder (PLH) umbrella, with USD activities handled through Unit's FDIC charter—providing full compliance without regulatory risk.
</Note>

**Key Partners:**
- **BitGo**: Cryptocurrency custody and compliance
- **Unit**: FDIC-insured banking infrastructure  
- **Plaid**: Account connection and verification
- **Privy**: Embedded wallet capabilities
