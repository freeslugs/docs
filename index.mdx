## What is Fellow?

Fellow is a **global financial router** - if you have a phone number, you already have a Fellow account.

We've built a productized financial platform for open asset communication and orchestration that routes money instantly between any financial app, wallet, or institution using simple phone numbers instead of complex account details. Fellow acts as the connective tissue between disconnected financial systems.

<Note>
  **Key Insight**: Fellow eliminates the friction between your financial platforms and gives you complete control over your financial timeline.
</Note>

## The Problem We Solve

<Warning>
  **Financial apps don't talk to each other.** Global finance is fragmented by platform.
</Warning>

The reality today:
- **Apple Pay to Coinbase** takes 5+ business days
- **Traditional cross-platform transfers** cost $25-50 in fees  
- **91% of consumers** maintain multiple financial apps due to feature gaps
- **Your money moves instantly** everywhere except between your own apps

Even 'instant' payments aren't actually instant. Venmo's "instant" transfer is really just an IOU—your money still takes 1-3 days to actually move via ACH.

## How Fellow Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Router Architecture">
    Fellow operates as a single financial router that provides connectivity between platforms in a consolidated financial experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cross-Platform Connectivity">
    The router integrates with traditional banking rails (ACH) and cryptocurrency networks, enabling seamless transfers between previously incompatible systems.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phone Number Identity">
    Your phone number serves as your universal financial identifier, abstracting away complex wallet addresses and account numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="True Instant Settlement">

  </Step>
