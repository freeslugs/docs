## Global finance is fragmented by platform.

Fellow is **a single financial router** that provides connectivity between platforms in a consolidated financial experience. If you have a phone number, you already have a Fellow account.

<Note>
  **Our Answer**: Fellow solves the intrinsic UX issues to value movement between platforms with end-to-end routing that connects any financial app, wallet, or institution.
</Note>

## Why Fellow Exists

**The Problem**: Your money gets trapped in silos that don't communicate. Each platform operates independently with no interoperability.

**The Reality Today:**
- **Apple Pay to Coinbase** takes 5+ business days
- **Traditional cross-platform transfers** cost $25-50 in fees  
- **91% of consumers** maintain multiple financial apps due to feature gaps
- **Your money moves instantly** everywhere except between your own apps

Even 'instant' payments aren't actually instant. Venmo's "instant" transfer is really just an IOU—your money still takes 1-3 days to actually move via ACH.
