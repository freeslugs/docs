Fellow operates on a simple, transparent fee structure with no hidden costs, monthly fees, or minimum balance requirements. Pay only when you use instant transfers, and earn superior returns when you provide liquidity.

<Info>
  **Our Philosophy**: One clear fee for instant transfers (40-80 basis points), with the majority flowing directly to liquidity providers who power the network.
</Info>

## Transfer Fees

Fellow charges **40-80 basis points** (0.40% - 0.80%) for instant cross-platform transfers, dramatically cheaper and faster than traditional alternatives.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="What You Pay" icon="receipt">
    **0.40% - 0.80%** of transfer amount
    No monthly fees, no minimums, no hidden costs
  </Card>
  <Card title="What You Get" icon="bolt">
    **Instant settlement** to crypto and traditional platforms, and universal connectivity
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Common Transfers">

    | Transfer Amount | Fellow Fee (60 bps) |
    | --------------- | ------------------- |
    | \$500           | \$3.00              |
    | \$1,000         | \$6.00              |
    | \$5,000         | \$30.00             |
    | \$10,000        | \$60.00             |

    <Check>
      **Example**: Moving 2,000 from Robinhood to your crypto wallet costs 12 and settles instantly vs. 1-3 business days for traditional transfers.
    </Check>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Fee Structure">
    **Current Pricing Model**

    Fellow currently charges a **flat rate within the 40-80 basis point range** for all instant transfers, keeping pricing simple and transparent.

    **What Determines Your Fee:**

    - Route complexity (crypto conversions, multi-platform routing)
    - Transfer amount and destination type
    - Network processing costs

    **Future Pricing Evolution:**
    As Fellow grows, we may introduce dynamic pricing based on factors like:

    - Network demand and liquidity utilization
    - User payment history and loyalty
    - Transfer volume and frequency
    - Route optimization and efficiency

    <Note>
      Any pricing changes will be announced in advance with clear explanations of the new structure.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### LP Earnings Breakdown

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Revenue Distribution">
    **Where Transfer Fees Go**

    ```
    User pays 60 basis points on $10,000 transfer = $60
    
    → Liquidity Providers: $45 (75%)
    → Protocol Operations: $9 (15%)  
    → Infrastructure Costs: $6 (10%)
    ```

    **LP Share Calculation**: If you own 2% of the liquidity pool, you receive 2% of the 45 LP portion =  0.90 from this single transfer.

    <Note>
      With thousands of transfers daily, these fees compound quickly for liquidity providers.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="APR Calculation">
    **Real Return Examples**

    **Conservative Scenario** (Low network activity):

    - Pool size: \$2M total liquidity
    - Your stake: \$10,000 (0.5% of pool)
    - Daily volume: \$200,000
    - Average fee: 50 basis points
    - Daily fees to LPs: \$750
    - Your daily earnings: \$3.75
    - **Annualized return: ~35% APR**

    **Active Scenario** (Moderate network activity):

    - Pool size: \$2M total liquidity
    - Your stake: \$10,000 (0.5% of pool)
    - Daily volume: \$500,000
    - Average fee: 60 basis points
    - Daily fees to LPs: \$2,250
    - Your daily earnings: \$11.25
    - **Annualized return: ~55% APR**

    - Easy withdrawals anytime

    **Optimal Utilization** (50-80% of pool active):

## Fee Transparency

Fellow operates with complete transparency - no hidden fees, surprise charges, or complex terms.

**What's Included in Transfer Fees:**

- Instant liquidity provision
- Cross-platform routing and settlement
- Credit assessment and risk management
- 24/7 technical infrastructure
- Customer support and dispute resolution

**What's Never Charged:**

- Monthly account maintenance fees
- Minimum balance penalties
- Inactivity fees
- Account closure fees
- Basic banking services (mobile deposit, bill pay, etc.)

<Check>
  **Our vision**: You only pay when you use instant transfers. Everything else is free.
</Check>
