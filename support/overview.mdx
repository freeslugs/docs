## Common Issues & Solutions

### Account Connection Problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="link" title="Can't Connect Bank Account">
    **Quick Fixes:**
    1. Verify your online banking credentials are correct
    2. Check if your bank requires additional verification
    3. Try connecting through your bank's mobile app first
    4. Contact your bank to ensure third-party connections are allowed
    
    **If Still Having Issues:**
    - Use the manual connection option with account/routing numbers
    - Contact support with your bank name for specific guidance
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion icon="coins" title="Crypto Wallet Connection Issues">
    **Common Solutions:**
    1. Ensure you have the correct API keys from your exchange
    2. Check that API permissions include "read balances" and "withdraw"
    3. For hardware wallets, ensure latest firmware is installed
    4. Verify WalletConnect is enabled for mobile wallets
    
    **Get Help:**
    - Support can walk you through API key generation
    - We provide guides for each major crypto platform
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Transfer Problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="clock" title="Transfer Taking Too Long">
    **Expected Times:**
    - Cross-platform transfers: <5 seconds
    - Same-platform transfers: Instant
    - First-time platform connections: Up to 30 seconds
    
    **If Delayed:**
    1. Check platform status pages for outages
    2. Verify sufficient balance in source account
    3. Ensure recipient information is correct
    4. Contact support if over 1 minute
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion icon="x" title="Transfer Failed">
    **Automatic Protection:**
    - Failed transfers automatically reverse
    - Money returns to source account within 5 minutes
    - No fees charged for failed transactions
    
    **Common Causes:**
    - Insufficient funds in source account
    - Platform maintenance or outages
    - Daily/monthly limits exceeded
    - Incorrect recipient information
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### App & Account Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="lock" title="Can't Access Account">
    **Account Recovery:**
    1. Try "Forgot PIN" option in app
    2. Verify phone number for SMS reset
    3. Check if biometric authentication needs re-setup
    4. Contact support for account unlock
    
    **Security Note:**
    - We'll verify your identity before account recovery
    - May require additional verification documents
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion icon="smartphone" title="App Not Working">
    **Troubleshooting Steps:**
    1. Force close and restart the Fellow app
    2. Check for app updates in App Store/Play Store
    3. Restart your phone
    4. Ensure stable internet connection
    5. Clear app cache (Android) or reinstall app
    
    **Still Having Issues:**
    - Report the specific error message to support
    - Include your phone model and OS version
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### If You Suspect Fraud

<Steps>
  <Step title="Secure Your Account">
    1. Change your Fellow PIN immediately
    2. Log out of all devices
    3. Check recent transaction history
    4. Screenshot any suspicious activity
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Contact Emergency Support">
    - **Call**: 1-800-FLW-HELP (24/7)
    - **Email**: security@fellow.app
    - **In-App**: Use "Report Security Issue" button
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Protect Connected Accounts">
    1. Change passwords on all connected financial accounts
    2. Enable 2FA where available
    3. Monitor all accounts for unusual activity
    4. Consider temporarily disconnecting accounts
  </Step>
</Steps>
