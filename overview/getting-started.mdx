## Step 2: Connecting Your Accounts

Connect your existing financial accounts to Fellow's router through secure integrations:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Traditional Banking">
    **Bank Account Connection**
    
    We use Plaid's secure API to connect your existing bank accounts:
    
    - **Security**: Bank-grade encryption with read-only access
    - **Speed**: Connection typically takes under 60 seconds
    - **Coverage**: Works with major banks, credit unions, and online banks
    
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Your Bank">
        Choose from thousands of supported financial institutions
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter Credentials">
        Securely log in through Plaid's encrypted interface
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose Accounts">
        Select which accounts you want to connect to Fellow
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm Connection">
        Verify account details and connection status
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Crypto Wallets">
    **Wallet Integration**
    
    Connect your crypto wallets by pasting wallet addresses:
    
    - **Multiple Networks**: Ethereum, Solana, Bitcoin, and more
    - **Any Wallet Type**: MetaMask, Phantom, hardware wallets, exchange wallets
    - **Simple Process**: Just paste your public wallet address
    
    <Warning>
    Only paste **public wallet addresses** - never share private keys or seed phrases with any service.
    </Warning>
    
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Network">
        Choose the blockchain network (Ethereum, Solana, etc.)
      </Step>
      <Step title="Paste Address">
        Copy and paste your public wallet address
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify Ownership">
        Sign a verification message with your wallet
      </Step>
      <Step title="Name Your Wallet">
        Give your wallet a memorable name for easy identification
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Exchanges & Brokerages">
    **Platform Connections**
    
    Link your trading and investment accounts:
    
    - **Crypto Exchanges**: Connect to major crypto trading platforms
    - **Brokerages**: Link investment and trading accounts
    - **Fintech Apps**: Connect digital payment and banking apps
    
    <Note>
    API connections provide secure access for balance checking and transfers. Each platform may have different connection requirements.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
**Need to see what's supported?** Check our complete [Platform Integrations](/using-fellow/supported-platforms) page for the full list of compatible financial services.
</Tip>

## Step 3: Your First Transfer

Ready to experience instant cross-platform transfers? Here's how to make your first transfer:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Choose Source" icon="arrow-up-from-bracket">
    Select which account you want to send money from
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Select Destination" icon="arrow-down-to-bracket">  
    Pick where you want the money to arrive instantly
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Confirm & Send" icon="paper-plane">
    Review the 40-80 basis point fee and confirm transfer
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Example Transfer Scenarios

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Crypto to Bank">
    **Scenario**: Moving profits from Coinbase to your checking account
    
    1. **Source**: Coinbase (select from connected accounts)
    2. **Amount**: $1,000 
    3. **Destination**: Chase Checking
    4. **Fee**: ~$4-8 (40-80 basis points)
    5. **Settlement**: Instant in Chase, 2-4 days for final ACH
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Bank to Crypto">
    **Scenario**: Funding your crypto wallet from savings
    
    1. **Source**: Wells Fargo Savings
    2. **Amount**: $500
    3. **Destination**: MetaMask Wallet  
    4. **Fee**: ~$2-4 (40-80 basis points)
    5. **Settlement**: Instant in MetaMask, 2-4 days for ACH
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Cross-Platform Trading">
    **Scenario**: Moving between brokerages for better rates
    
    1. **Source**: Robinhood Account
    2. **Amount**: $2,000
    3. **Destination**: Schwab Trading Account
    4. **Fee**: ~$8-16 (40-80 basis points)  
    5. **Settlement**: Instant access in Schwab
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Check>
**Success!** You've just made an instant transfer that would normally take 1-3 business days.
</Check>
