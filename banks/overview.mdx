# Transform Your Bank into a Financial Hub

Traditional banks face an existential challenge: customers increasingly live across multiple financial platforms, but banks can only serve their own ecosystem. Fellow solves this by making your bank the central hub for your customers' entire financial lives.

<Note>

## The Challenge Banks Face Today

### Customer Fragmentation

<Warning>
  **The Reality**: 91% of your customers use multiple financial apps because no single platform meets all their needs. They're forced to maintain accounts across traditional banks, neobanks, crypto exchanges, investment platforms, and payment apps.
</Warning>

**What This Means for Banks:**
- **Lower engagement**: Customers only visit your app for basic banking
- **Reduced deposits**: Money flows to higher-yield alternatives  
- **Customer churn**: Younger customers choose crypto-native or neobank platforms
- **Limited growth**: Constrained to traditional banking products and services

### The Infrastructure Problem

Building cross-platform connectivity requires:
- **Years of development** and millions in investment
- **Complex partnerships** with hundreds of financial platforms
- **Regulatory compliance** across multiple jurisdictions
- **Ongoing maintenance** as platforms change and evolve

**Most banks can't justify this investment.** Fellow provides the solution.
